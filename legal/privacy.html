1. What stays on your device

Your learning progress, settings and favorites are stored only in your browser (local storage and cache). We do not receive them. Clearing your browser data removes them; use the export function in the app to back them up.

2. Essays sent for AI feedback

When you press "start" on the writing feedback page, the essay and optional question you entered are sent through our server to a third-party AI provider, DeepSeek, to generate feedback. We do not store the text and do not write it to our logs. The provider processes it under its own terms and privacy policy, and its servers may be located outside your country, including in China. You must tick a consent box before anything is sent. Do not submit sensitive personal information.

3. Usage limits

To enforce daily limits we compute a one-way hash of your IP address combined with a secret value, and keep a counter against it for up to 48 hours. We do not store your raw IP address in our own database.

4. Hosting

The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes technical data such as IP addresses and request logs to deliver and secure the site. We do not run advertising or third-party analytics trackers.

5. Payments

When paid plans are available, payments are handled by Paddle as merchant of record. We do not receive your card number. We may receive limited purchase information such as your email address, country and plan.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

We do not use advertising cookies. We use local storage and a service-worker cache to make the app work and to allow offline use.

7. Sharing

We share data only with the providers described above, and only as needed to run the Service. We do not sell personal data.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. We hold very little data about you; to make a request, email {{supportEmail}}.

9. Children

The Service is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
